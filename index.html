Why It Matters

Charlotte is growing fast.
Our neighbors need support.

1 in 8 Charlotte residents face food insecurity. 35% are cost-burdened by housing. Over 200,000 lack health insurance. QueenCityConnect exists to connect people to the help that's already here.
