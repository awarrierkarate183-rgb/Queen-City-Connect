Our Mission

Connecting schools, neighborhoods, and communities to help nearby

Charlotte is one of the fastest-growing cities in the US, but that growth brings real challenges. Thousands of residents struggle to find food, housing, mental health care, and other essential services.

QueenCityConnect is a free, searchable directory of local nonprofits and community programs, all in one place, available 24/7.

Charlotte Today

The Need Is Real

Data from the U.S. Census Bureau, Feeding America, and UNC Charlotte Urban Institute.

1 in 8 Face Food Insecurity

Over 112,000 Charlotte-area residents don't know where their next meal is coming from.

35% Cost-Burdened by Housing

More than one-third of Charlotte residents spend over 30% of their income on housing.

200K+ Lack Health Insurance

Hundreds of thousands of Mecklenburg County residents rely on free and low-cost health clinics.

#2 Fastest Growing City

Charlotte's rapid growth puts pressure on community services across Mecklenburg County.
